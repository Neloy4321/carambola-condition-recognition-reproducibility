# Historical figures and reporting caveats

The figures in this folder were exported from the completed historical experimental workflow (paper-v2 Drive results). They are included for audit and visualization, **not** as fresh biological validation. The source photograph montage and Grad-CAM photographic examples are excluded pending image-release permissions. Learning curves and confusion/calibration/robustness graphs may be revisited for manuscript use after matching source result versions and claims.

The earlier model-comparison statistics are image-level and could be optimistic when multiple images come from the same plant or capture group. Synthetic corruptions are not real orchard/domain validation. The calibration, risk-coverage and external-class evaluations were already inspected and are exploratory.
