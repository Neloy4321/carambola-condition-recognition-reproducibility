# Limitations and interpretation register

- Biological independence (tree, orchard and specimen) **unverified**; capture-proxy groups are provisional.
- Proxy-group and original class are confounded in much of the grouped test.
- Original split test n=388 and grouped proxy test n=394 are not comparable as a single paired experiment. Only 59 images overlap.
- Two partial-class online external evaluations were poor (15.52%, 28.70%) and have been inspected.
- Earlier expert review stages: 231 image-file records, model-error enriched; additional blind review: 72 selected training records by one reviewer. Not a population-representative survey of label errors.
- The two expert-review rounds combined do not establish 303 independent specimens; no multi-expert inter-rater reliability is available.
- Review discrepancies are about visual patterns; Fe nutrient deficiency lacks laboratory or field verification.
- Source dataset count 2,416 in some older records vs audited 2,644 files / 2,583 MD5-unique remains unresolved.
- Earlier QA counted 20 reviewed proxy groups, but latest stage-1/2 source manifest yields 19. Preserve both without inventing reconciliation.
- Three suspected Stage-3 repeat pairs are unresolved; only proxy groups were used.
- The strict 3-correction label overlay is advisory; no full three-seed corrected-label ResNet50V2 model has been run.
- The small 72-image image-feature pilot did **not** demonstrate improvement. It is not independent evaluation.
- Retrospective temperature scaling, conformal sets and confidence referral were assessed on previously inspected data, not a fresh prospective cohort.
- Model weights, source photos, signed certificates and private crosswalks remain outside the public repository.
- Provenance, rights, consent, exact authorship/affiliations and chosen journal's policies need explicit final confirmation.

Negative and inconclusive findings are preserved rather than omitted.
